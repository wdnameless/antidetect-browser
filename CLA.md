## 1. Why NullTrace Uses a CLA (Dual Licensing Purpose)

NullTrace is distributed under a **dual-licensing business model**:
1. An open-source edition licensed under the **GNU Affero General Public License, Version 3 (AGPL-3.0)**.
2. A proprietary commercial license (**`LICENSE.COMMERCIAL`**) for enterprise clients and organizations requiring exemption from AGPL-3.0 copyleft and network-disclosure terms.

To lawfully offer a proprietary commercial license for the entire software package without infringing contributor copyrights, the Operator must hold broad licensing rights to all incoming contributions. 

> **Note on DCO vs. CLA:** A Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO, `Signed-off-by`) confirms only that the contributor authored the patch and submits it under the existing open-source license (AGPL-3.0). A DCO **does not** give the Operator legal authority to relicense the contribution under the separate commercial license. Therefore, a CLA is strictly necessary to sustain NullTrace's dual-licensing structure.
